Hi, I'm Harjot Singh Raith, an AI and Data Science undergraduate with strong interests in AI systems, Large Language Models, qualitative research automation, and intelligent scheduling systems. I am currently a Research Intern at IIT Bombay (eYantra, Embedded Real-Time Systems Lab), where I work on building AI-driven tools for thematic analysis, document understanding, and educational data mining, bridging research methodology with real-world deployment.

My work spans LLM-powered research platforms, real-time systems, constraint-based optimization, and robotics, with publications and accepted papers in areas such as OCR-based video analysis, collaborative problem solving, timetable optimization, and RAG-based systems. I enjoy building end-to-end systems—from FastAPI backends and React dashboards to ML pipelines and embedded robotic platforms.

Beyond research, I serve as the Head of the Robotics Club at NMIMS Navi Mumbai, have won the Judges' Choice Award at IIT Bombay, and secured multiple wins across national and international hackathons. I'm always open to collaborating on impactful research, AI products, and interdisciplinary projects that push the boundaries of intelligent systems.
